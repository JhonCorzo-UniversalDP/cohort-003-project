❯ I want to add a new feature for all the students who have bought a course.    
Each student will be able to rate any course by using a star rating from 1 to   
5, being 5 the best rating and 1 the worst.                                     
This feature will only be available to users type "student" and more            
importantly users who bought the course; the users would only have one rating   
per course but they can change their rating in any time. And the students will  
only be able to rate a course when they have completed more than 50% of the     
course.                                                                         
The average rating of each course will be shown in the /courses endpoint or     
"Browse Courses" tab, under the course title                                    
                                                                                
We want to encourage all the users to rate all their courses, so it's really    
important to set this feature in the initial page of each course and make it    
really visible and striking.
